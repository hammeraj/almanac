Create one home-cooked meal for a single adult.

Fixed constraints (never break these):
- Serves exactly 1 person. Do not write a recipe meant for a household or that requires halving.
- Total hands-on prep and cook time is under 30 minutes. If something needs to marinate or chill overnight, that waiting time doesn't count, but say so explicitly.
- No onions in any form (fresh, powder, dried, or as part of a pre-made sauce/broth that lists onion).
- No green peppers (bell or otherwise) in any form.
- Ordinary grocery-store ingredients only — nothing that requires a specialty store.
- Realistic, appetizing, and simple enough to actually cook on a weeknight.

Tell me:
- Meal type: [breakfast / lunch / dinner / snack]
- Target calories: [number or range, aim for something that fits inside an 1800-2400 kcal/day diet]
- Target protein: [number or range in grams — lean toward higher protein]
- Anything else to avoid or prefer: [optional — other allergies, a cuisine preference, ingredients to use up, etc.]

Output format — return ONLY a single JavaScript object literal in this exact shape, ready to paste as a new entry into a RECIPE_LIBRARY object keyed by a short kebab-case id:

'kebab-case-id': {
  name:'Recipe Name', meal:'breakfast', kcal:000, protein:00, prepMin:00, servings:1,
  ingredients:['quantity + ingredient', 'quantity + ingredient', ...],
  steps:['Step one.', 'Step two.', ...]
}

Rules for the output:
- `meal` must be exactly one of: 'breakfast', 'lunch', 'dinner', 'snack'.
- `kcal` and `protein` are your best realistic estimate given the ingredient quantities — don't just invent round numbers, make them consistent with what's actually listed.
- `prepMin` is the total active time in minutes, and must be 30 or less.
- `servings` is always 1.
- `ingredients` are written as "quantity + ingredient" strings (e.g. "6 oz chicken breast, cubed"), specific enough that a grocery list could be built from them later.
- `steps` are short, imperative, numbered by array order — no step numbers inside the text itself.
- Double-check the finished recipe contains no onion and no green pepper before returning it.
- No commentary before or after the object — just the object literal, so it can be pasted directly into the app's source.
